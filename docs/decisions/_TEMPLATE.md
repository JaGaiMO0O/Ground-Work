## Context

What forced the decision. The constraint, the cost, or the thing that broke -
with numbers where there are numbers. Name the alternatives that were genuinely
considered, not a strawman, and say what is right about each of them.

An ADR nobody could have disagreed with was not worth writing.

## Decision

The choice, stated in one line, then the reasons in the order they actually
mattered. If one reason decided it on its own, say so - that is the most useful
sentence in the file.

## Consequences

**Good.** What this buys.

**Costs.** What it gives up, and what it makes harder. A consequences section
with no costs in it means the alternatives were never real.

**Open.** Anything this decision creates and does not close, recorded here
rather than discovered later.
